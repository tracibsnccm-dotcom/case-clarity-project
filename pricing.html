How to Maximize Your Investment

To get the most value from Case Clarity Project, we recommend following a structured assessment schedule. Each instrument provides critical insights at different stages of case development, and running them at the right frequency ensures you capture important trajectory changes when they matter most.

Recommended Follow-Up Schedule

Tool Description First re-run (no later than) Best Practice
CCI (Case Complexity Index) Assesses case complexity level and identifies key drivers Level 1 (critical: coma, ICU, paralysis, severe brain injury): every 2–7 days
Level 2: weekly
Level 3: every 10–14 days
Levels 4–5: every 21–30 days		 Higher complexity requires tighter monitoring. Catching complexity shifts early prevents case drift.

Important Context:

Each report gives its own re-run date as a calendar date. After two reports in a row show the case is stable, the longer interval on the report applies. It should be expected that these reports would—and should—be run often during the early phases of injury, treatment, and care. Early and frequent assessments capture the trajectory of decline and recovery when it matters most.

You have to run them often enough when the client is at their most vulnerable so that you can catch declines in condition before they undermine the case and the evidence you need to prove how hindered the client was from returning to baseline physically and financially.
